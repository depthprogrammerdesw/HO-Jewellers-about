Founder & Operator

From a livelihood-driven beginning to a trusted jewellery store, HO Jewellers continues to grow through personal attention, quality-focused work and customer trust.

Mr. Nishant Kumar Burman founded the store in 2017 with the aim of creating a sustainable livelihood and building something valuable for the long term. Seeing the potential for growth in the jewellery market, he steadily developed the store into a trusted place for customers.
